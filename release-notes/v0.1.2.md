# 案卷观察台 v0.1.2 完整社区版

`v0.1.2` 是全库检索、历史直播文字和 AI 研究工作流更新。安装包将继续完整携带公开发布基线，不改为首次启动后再下载历史资料的轻量客户端。

## 主要更新

- 新增 AI Chat：先检索本地法院案卷、直播全文、案件时间线、人物公司、实体关系、政策资料和现有法律解读，再由用户选择的 Ollama 或云端模型生成带证据编号的回答。
- AI Chat 对话历史仅保存在当前电脑，支持新建、搜索、重命名、删除、继续追问和中英文混合提问。
- 增加证据引用、法院认定/当事人主张/公开言论分类与引用校验；当轻量模型错误归类检方立场或使用无效证据编号时，程序拒绝展示该答案。
- 内置 2017-2023 历史公开言论和直播文字检索：5,151 条目录记录，5,097 份可检索全文，5,097 份英文记录，共 145,763,802 个英文译文字符。
- 完整法律资料基线更新为 1,867 条清单记录、1,840 份有效 PDF、1,797 份按内容去重的 PDF 正文和 16,837 个发布种子缓存文件。
- 搜索按 PDF 字节哈希和案卷身份去重，保留同一内容的备用来源，并支持引号短语、人名别名、案号和 PDF 正文关键词。
- 后台优先更新改为仅处理本轮新下载或变更的文件，不再每 30 分钟轮换重处理已完成的历史资料；用户明确选择全部处理时仍可完整重建。
- GitHub Release 每个安装包强制低于 1.969 GiB，为 GitHub 2 GiB 单文件上限预留 32 MiB 传输余量；超限时构建直接失败，不会静默删除案卷、译文、解读或直播全文。

## 资料质量边界

全部 1,797 份唯一 PDF 都有可搜索的本地初读基线，但不等于全部都由律师逐页审阅。当前 88 份具有与 PDF SHA-256 绑定的专业双语编辑复核，其余主要是本地确定性规则初读或未审阅生成结果。中文译文中有 67 份是审阅级完整译文，大部分其他文件为辅助阅读译文。界面会保留质量标签，不把辅助结果冒充为经专业人工审核的法律意见。

PACER 仍是正式案卷记录。本项目不承诺已获得密封、受限、撤下、未上传 RECAP 或尚未公开的 PACER 文件。AI 输出是研究辅助，不是律师代理或正式法律意见。

# Docket Observatory v0.1.2 Complete Community Edition

`v0.1.2` expands whole-library search, historical broadcast transcripts, and the AI-assisted research workflow. The installers continue to bundle the complete public release baseline rather than becoming small clients that download the historical library after first launch.

## Highlights

- Added AI Chat, which retrieves local court records, full transcripts, timelines, people and companies, entity relationships, policy records, and existing legal readings before a user-selected Ollama or cloud model prepares a cited answer.
- Local conversation history supports creation, search, rename, deletion, follow-up questions, and Chinese or English prompts.
- Added evidence-class and citation validation. Answers are rejected when a lightweight model misattributes a prosecution position or cites an invalid evidence identifier.
- Bundled the 2017-2023 public-statement and transcript corpus: 5,151 catalog records, 5,097 searchable transcripts, and 5,097 English records containing 145,763,802 translated characters.
- Updated the legal baseline to 1,867 manifest records, 1,840 valid PDFs, 1,797 content-unique PDF bodies, and 16,837 seed-cache files.
- Improved byte-hash and docket-identity deduplication while retaining alternate source links, quoted phrases, aliases, docket numbers, and PDF-body search.
- Scheduled priority updates now process only files downloaded or changed in the current run. They no longer rotate through completed historical documents every 30 minutes. Explicit full processing remains available.
- Every GitHub Release installer must remain below 1.969 GiB, reserving 32 MiB beneath GitHub's 2 GiB single-file limit. Oversized builds fail instead of silently removing court records, translations, readings, or transcripts.

## Quality Boundary

All 1,797 unique PDF bodies have a searchable local first-reading baseline, but this does not mean that every document received page-by-page lawyer review. Eighty-eight PDFs have SHA-256-bound professional bilingual editorial review; most remaining files use deterministic local-rule first reads or unreviewed generated output. Sixty-seven Chinese translations are reviewed and complete, while most other Chinese outputs are assistive reading translations. The interface preserves these quality labels and does not present assistive material as professionally reviewed legal advice.

PACER remains the docket of record. This project does not claim access to sealed, restricted, removed, not-yet-uploaded-to-RECAP, or otherwise nonpublic PACER documents. AI output is research assistance, not attorney representation or formal legal advice.
