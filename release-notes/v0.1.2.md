# 案卷观察台 v0.1.2

`v0.1.2` 是全库检索、历史直播文字、GHOT 文字档案和 AI 研究工作流更新。三个桌面安装包继续完整携带公开发布基线，不改为首次启动后再下载历史资料的轻量客户端。

## 同版本 BUG 修复

本次仍使用 `0.1.2`，不改变应用版本号。修复 AI Chat 的上下文污染、跨年份证据选择、模型引用声明、无依据机构身份扩写、主体关系跨句拼接、中文数量识别和普通对话/资料研究分流问题；同时修复 PDF 搜索缓存版本同步、稀疏 PDF 的本地 OCR 降级处理，以及更新分析结果排序的问题。无模型时仍只检索本地资料，接入 Ollama 或云端模型后才进行回答综合。

## 主要更新

- 新增全库研究 / AI Chat：先检索本地法院案卷、直播全文、GHOT 名词和法庭文件档案、案件时间线、人物公司、实体关系、政策资料和现有法律解读，再由用户选择的 Ollama 或云端模型生成带证据编号的回答。无模型时仍提供本地检索和档案模式，但不进行生成式整合、联想或推理。
- AI Chat 对话历史仅保存在当前电脑，支持新建、搜索、重命名、删除、继续追问和中英文混合提问。
- 增加证据引用、法院认定/当事人主张/公开言论分类与引用校验；当轻量模型错误归类检方立场或使用无效证据编号时，程序拒绝展示该答案。
- 内置 2017-2023 历史公开言论和直播文字检索：5,152 条目录记录，5,098 份可检索全文，5,098 份英文记录，共 145,768,820 个英文译文字符。
- 内置 375 条 GHOT 公开文字档案记录，包括 365 条法庭文件摘要和 7 条名词/概念资料，并保留中英文详情和二级档案边界。
- 完整法律资料基线更新为 1,924 条清单记录、1,897 份有效 PDF、1,846 份按内容去重的 PDF 正文和 17,272 个发布种子缓存文件。
- 搜索按 PDF 字节哈希和案卷身份去重，保留同一内容的备用来源，并支持引号短语、人名别名、案号和 PDF 正文关键词。
- 后台优先更新改为仅处理本轮新下载或变更的文件，不再每 30 分钟轮换重处理已完成的历史资料；用户明确选择全部处理时仍可完整重建。
- GitHub Release 每个安装包强制低于 1.969 GiB，为 GitHub 2 GiB 单文件上限预留 32 MiB 传输余量；超限时构建直接失败，不会静默删除案卷、译文、解读或直播全文。

## 资料质量边界

全部 1,846 份唯一 PDF 都有双语文件阅读记录，但不等于全部都由律师逐页审阅。当前 99 份具有与 PDF SHA-256 绑定的专业复核；逻辑文件中 1,295 份主要使用本地确定性规则初读。中文译文中有 72 份达到审阅级完整翻译，大部分其他文件为辅助阅读译文。界面会保留质量标签，不把辅助结果冒充为经专业人工审核的法律意见。

自动风险审计标记的 245 份文件均已有发布者决定，245/245 已解决，发布门禁通过。决定记录来源、完整性与收录政策，不表示密封、保密、隐私或删节字样失去法律效力，也不等同于逐页人工法律审阅。详见 [风险审计摘要](https://github.com/Dysen177/docket-observatory/blob/v0.1.2/release-metadata/corpus-risk-audit.md)。

PACER 仍是正式案卷记录。本项目不承诺已获得密封、受限、撤下、未上传 RECAP 或尚未公开的 PACER 文件。AI 输出是研究辅助，不是律师代理或正式法律意见。

# Docket Observatory v0.1.2

`v0.1.2` expands whole-library search, historical broadcast transcripts, and the AI-assisted research workflow. The installers continue to bundle the complete public release baseline rather than becoming small clients that download the historical library after first launch.

## Same-Version Bug Fix

This is still `0.1.2`; the application version is unchanged. The update fixes AI Chat context contamination, cross-year evidence selection, model citation declarations, unsupported institutional-role expansion, cross-sentence subject relationships, Chinese quantity parsing, and conversation/research routing. It also synchronizes the PDF search-cache version, preserves sparse PDF text when local OCR is unavailable, and improves analysis-result ordering. Without a model the app remains local-search only; Ollama or a cloud model is required for synthesis.

## Highlights

- Added AI Chat, which retrieves local court records, full transcripts, timelines, people and companies, entity relationships, policy records, and existing legal readings before a user-selected Ollama or cloud model prepares a cited answer.
- Local conversation history supports creation, search, rename, deletion, follow-up questions, and Chinese or English prompts.
- Added evidence-class and citation validation. Answers are rejected when a lightweight model misattributes a prosecution position or cites an invalid evidence identifier.
- Bundled the 2017-2023 public-statement and transcript corpus: 5,152 catalog records, 5,098 searchable transcripts, and 5,098 English records containing 145,768,820 translated characters.
- Added a bundled bilingual GHOT public text archive with 375 records, including 365 court-filing summaries and 7 terminology/concept records.
- Updated the legal baseline to 1,924 manifest records, 1,897 valid PDFs, 1,846 content-unique PDF bodies, and 17,272 seed-cache files.
- Improved byte-hash and docket-identity deduplication while retaining alternate source links, quoted phrases, aliases, docket numbers, and PDF-body search.
- Scheduled priority updates now process only files downloaded or changed in the current run. They no longer rotate through completed historical documents every 30 minutes. Explicit full processing remains available.
- Every GitHub Release installer must remain below 1.969 GiB, reserving 32 MiB beneath GitHub's 2 GiB single-file limit. Oversized builds fail instead of silently removing court records, translations, readings, or transcripts.

## Quality Boundary

All 1,846 unique PDF bodies have bilingual document-reading records, but this does not mean that every document received page-by-page lawyer review. Ninety-nine PDFs have SHA-256-bound professional review; 1,295 logical documents primarily use deterministic local-rule first reads. Seventy-two Chinese translations are reviewed and complete, while most other Chinese outputs are assistive reading translations. The interface preserves these quality labels and does not present assistive material as professionally reviewed legal advice.

Publisher decisions exist for all 245 files flagged by the automated risk audit, leaving zero unresolved items and passing the packaging gate. A decision records provenance, integrity, and inclusion policy; it does not nullify sealing, confidentiality, privacy, or redaction language and is not page-by-page legal review. See the [risk-audit summary](https://github.com/Dysen177/docket-observatory/blob/v0.1.2/release-metadata/corpus-risk-audit.md).

PACER remains the docket of record. This project does not claim access to sealed, restricted, removed, not-yet-uploaded-to-RECAP, or otherwise nonpublic PACER documents. AI output is research assistance, not attorney representation or formal legal advice.
